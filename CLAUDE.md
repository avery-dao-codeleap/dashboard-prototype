# Project Notes

## Track Separation

**`no-product-management/`** — SDK-only track. Nothing webstore-related belongs here. May or may not include SDK product management (TBD), but webstore catalog, webstore rules, and any webstore-specific UI are strictly out of scope for this track. This track also develops its **Transactions** page independently — starting with SDK transactions only (in-app purchases), with no webstore transaction data in scope initially.

**`with-product-management/`** — Full dashboard including both SDK and Webstore product management tracks.
